Drawably

Hand-drawn UI controls. Every mount generates a fresh pen sketch from a seed, and the stroke boils like an animated doodle.

Outline, solid, and scribble buttons, plus a checkbox, a toggle, and a short phrase marked with underline, highlight, and a circle. Roughness is how wobbly the line is; boil is the flicker. Lock a seed or resketch for another drawing.
